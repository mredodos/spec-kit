# Writing Principles

Copy or adapt this template into your project's writing principles (e.g. `.universe/memory/writing-principles.md` or your own doc).

- **Quality**: Keep structure and content clear and documented.
- **Consistency**: Align names, rules, and tone across the universe and series.
- **Simplicity**: Prefer the simplest structure that serves the story.
- **Scope**: Set realistic goals for drafts and revisions; use the framework's tools to stay organised.
